## Current Customer-Facing Brand

- Customer-facing business name: Moto & Co Couriers.
- Parent company: Moto & Co Pty Ltd.
- Tagline: The Motorcycle Supply Chain. Simplified.
- Category: motorcycle last mile logistics.
- Core positioning: specialist logistics connecting motorcycle suppliers and workshops through scheduled delivery services.
- Primary workshop message: keep your technicians on the tools.
- Primary supplier message: extend your delivery network, not your fleet.

## Marketing Capability Rule

Moto & Co Couriers needs a controlled marketing and copy review capability. This capability should check that public copy:

- uses Moto & Co Couriers as the customer-facing brand name;
- uses Moto & Co only when referring to the parent company;
- uses motorcycle supply chain, last mile logistics, Standard Network Rates, Workshop Partner, Supplier Partner, delivery network, and specialist logistics language where suitable;
- avoids cheap, parcel delivery, freight company, transport solutions, one-stop shop, and over-corporate generic logistics language;
- keeps pricing, supplier list, delivery cadence, proof of delivery, billing, service area, and portal promises aligned to the approved operating baseline;
- uses supplier warehouse cut-off/readiness wording instead of a fixed Moto & Co-owned order cut-off time;
- keeps internal control terms out of customer-facing pages.

## Public Copy Boundaries

Customer-facing website copy may describe:

- scheduled delivery days;
- supplier warehouse cut-off/readiness expectations;
- participating suppliers;
- customer portal access;
- electronic proof of delivery;
- Standard Network Rates;
- monthly PDF invoicing;
- workshop and supplier partnership outcomes.

Customer-facing website copy must not expose:

- internal baseline, version, or document-control language;
- BOAS, SOP, policy, administration, or source-control terminology;
- draft labels, future roadmap brands, or unapproved service categories;
- unapproved pricing placeholders;
- unapproved phone, address, service-region, or proof-point claims.
