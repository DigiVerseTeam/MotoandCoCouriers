## Public-Facing Draft Copy

### Driver Safety and Respectful Conduct Policy

Effective June 2026 - Moto & Co Pty Ltd ABN 55 679 964 357

#### Our position

Moto and Co Couriers is committed to providing a safe and respectful working environment for drivers and delivery personnel.

We do not accept abuse, threats, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, aggression, sexual harassment, unsafe conduct, or violence toward any person carrying out work for Moto and Co Couriers.

Zero tolerance means this behaviour is not part of the service and may result in immediate action to protect driver safety.

#### Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to all people who interact with Moto and Co Couriers drivers or delivery personnel, including:

- workshop customers;
- supplier staff;
- receivers at delivery addresses;
- site visitors, contractors, or other people present at a pickup or delivery location;
- anyone communicating with us about a booking, pickup, delivery, account, dispute, or invoice.

#### Behaviour we do not accept

The following conduct is not accepted:

- verbal abuse, shouting, swearing at a person, or personal insults;
- threats, intimidation, coercion, or attempts to pressure a driver into unsafe work;
- physical aggression, assault, blocking movement, or interfering with a vehicle or goods;
- harassment, bullying, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, or retaliatory behaviour;
- sexual harassment, sexual comments, sexual gestures, or any unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature;
- discriminatory, racist, sexist, homophobic, ableist, or otherwise degrading comments or behaviour;
- unsafe behaviour at a pickup or delivery location, including unsafe vehicle movement, unsafe loading areas, unsecured hazards, or instructions to enter an unsafe area;
- abusive phone, email, portal, or message contact.

Reasonable questions, complaints, delivery queries, billing queries, or service feedback are not abuse when they are raised respectfully.

#### Driver authority to stop unsafe work

A driver may stop a pickup, delivery, or interaction if they reasonably believe it is unsafe or inappropriate to continue.

This may include leaving the location, staying in a safe area, contacting Moto and Co Couriers, recording the job as unable to collect or unable to deliver, or contacting emergency services if there is an immediate risk.

Drivers are not required to continue an interaction where they are being abused, threatened, harassed, intimidated, or exposed to an unsafe site condition.

#### Safe access and site conditions

Customers, suppliers, and receivers must provide safe and reasonable access for collection and delivery.

If a pickup or delivery location presents an immediate safety risk, the driver may refuse to enter the area or may stop the job until the risk is resolved.

If a supplier location has an unresolved safety hazard, Moto and Co Couriers may refuse further attendance at that location until we are satisfied the hazard has been addressed.

#### What may happen after unacceptable conduct

Where unacceptable conduct or unsafe site conditions are reported, Moto and Co Couriers may take one or more of the following steps:

- pause, cancel, or refuse the affected pickup or delivery;
- require a different site contact or delivery arrangement;
- restrict communication to a nominated business contact;
- suspend portal access or account services;
- end a customer or supplier relationship;
- report serious threats, assault, sexual harassment, or criminal conduct to police or another appropriate authority;
- cooperate with emergency services, workplace health and safety authorities, or other lawful investigations where required.

Charges may still apply where a pickup or delivery cannot proceed because of unacceptable conduct or unsafe site conditions, to the extent permitted by law and our Booking Terms.

#### Reports and records

Moto and Co Couriers may record driver safety reports, site safety concerns, communication records, and any steps taken in response.

We use these records to manage safety, investigate incidents, protect workers and other people, decide whether service can continue, and meet legal or operational obligations.

We handle personal information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and only share information where necessary for safety, legal, emergency, or business purposes.

#### No retaliation

We do not accept retaliation against a person who reports a safety concern, harassment, abuse, or unsafe conduct.

Any retaliation may be treated as a further breach of this policy.

#### Relationship with other terms

This policy should be read together with our Booking Terms, Delivery Disclaimer, Privacy Policy, and any account terms that apply to your service.

Nothing in this policy limits any legal right, obligation, or remedy that cannot lawfully be limited.
